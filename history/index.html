The History of R Theory

A public timeline of Kit Cosby's R Theory — from early writings through the manuscripts, the Facebook record, the independent audit, and the forum being built as its home.

Compiled September 2026 from Kit's public Facebook posts and the R-Theory group. Each entry links to the original post.

Prelude

Before the theory had a name.

  1. 2015
    A public manifesto

    Kit publishes "A Manifesto of an American Communist" on Tumblr — his earliest long-form public writing. Revised in 2026 with dated material removed and a new land-and-cattle program added.

  2. Dec 2024 – Jul 2025
    The cyclical-universe model

    Working notes on a cyclical cosmology — "Unaexpon" / "Unainverex" equations, space/anti-space and energy/anti-energy duality — including a prepared JCAP submission package. From the archived working sessions; relation to R Theory still open.

  3. Jan – Mar 2025
    First version of the theory completed

    Kit finishes the first version of R Theory over the winter season and begins looking for a place to put it — the origin of the forum project that became Lampy.
